# Groktopus Relationship Management

You leave a good conversation thinking, “I should introduce her to someone.” A month later, you remember the promise but not the name. The meeting is in your calendar, the name is in a transcript, and the friend who could make the connection is in a note you forgot you wrote.

We're starting GRM to put those pieces back together. It’s a personal CRM for one person, hosted on your own hardware, with your own assistant as the front door. Tell your assistant how the conversation went. Later, ask who you know who’s worked on a problem, who could make an introduction, or what you said you’d do next.

The point isn’t another place to check. It’s to remember where you left off with people and have a better reason to reach out than “it’s been a while.” GRM won’t send the message for you.

This is the beginning, not a release. We’re [researching the design](docs/design-considerations.md) before writing the software. If you want to help shape it, start with the [contribution guide](CONTRIBUTING.md).
